# CVE-2025-14547 — Integer Underflow in PSA Crypto EC-JPAKE ZKP Parsing

An integer underflow vulnerability is present in Silicon Labs' implementation of PSA Crypto and SE Manager EC-JPAKE APIs during ZKP parsing. Triggering the underflow can lead to a hard fault, causing a temporary denial of service.
